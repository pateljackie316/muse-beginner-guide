# Muse Beginner's Guide

> [中文版 (Chinese version)](README.md)

> A beginner's guide for anyone just getting started with Muse: first understand what it is, then take care of the single most important thing within 48 hours of signing up.

## 1. What Is Muse?

Muse is Meta's Personal AI Agent. Think of it as an AI assistant with its own computer: it doesn't just chat with you — it can actually roll up its sleeves and get things done for you.

For example, it can help you:

- ✈️ Compare flights and plan trips: tell it your origin, destination, and dates, and it will compare prices and times across different flights.
- 📝 Fill in forms: handle repetitive tasks such as filling out web forms and organizing sign-up information, exactly as you ask.
- 📄 Make documents: draft, format, and compile materials into documents you can use right away.
- ⏰ Set up scheduled tasks: hand it things that need to happen regularly, and it will remind you or run them on time.

In short: you give the instructions, it does the legwork. When you're starting out, begin with one small task (for example, organizing a list or comparing a flight) to learn how it takes instructions and reports back — then gradually hand it more complex work.

## 2. The Most Important First Step for Beginners: Redeem an Invite Code Within 48 Hours

After signing up for a Muse account, **redeem an invite code within 48 hours**. This is the most important — and the easiest to miss — step for beginners: the redemption entry only appears during the 48 hours after signup. Once it expires, the entry disappears and cannot be recovered.

Where to redeem:

- 📱 Mobile app: **Settings > Redeem token**
- 🌐 Web: **Settings > General > Usage > Redeem invite code**

When redeeming: open the entry, enter the invite code as prompted, and confirm. If the entry is already gone, the 48-hour window has passed.

## 4. A Few Practical Tips for Beginners

1. Start with one small task: pick something simple the first time to learn its working rhythm.
2. Be specific with your instructions: the clearer the time, place, budget, and format requirements, the closer the result will match your expectations.
3. Hand it recurring tasks: things that need regular reminders or execution are good candidates for its scheduled-task feature.
4. Double-check sensitive actions: when accounts, payments, or other sensitive information is involved, verify the page and the request before authorizing.
